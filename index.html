01 / Who we are

Economics is everywhere.
Understanding should be too.

Ringgitonomics is a Malaysian digital media initiative focused on making the forces shaping our economy more understandable and relevant.

From national policy to the decisions businesses make every day, we connect the numbers to the people and ideas behind them.

Our aim is to make financial and economic conversations more accessible without losing the substance. We bring context to the headlines, explain the implications behind the data, and create content that helps readers stay informed.
